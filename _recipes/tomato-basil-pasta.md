## Ingredients

- 1 lb pasta (spaghetti or linguine)
- 2 lbs fresh tomatoes, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Parmesan cheese for serving
- Red pepper flakes (optional)

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Cook pasta according to package directions.

2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.

3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

4. Add diced tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Cook for 10-15 minutes until tomatoes break down.

5. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain pasta.

6. Add pasta to the tomato sauce. Toss to combine, adding pasta water as needed to create a silky sauce.

7. Remove from heat and stir in fresh basil.

8. Serve with grated Parmesan and red pepper flakes if desired.

## Notes

- Use the best tomatoes you can find. In winter, canned San Marzano tomatoes work well.
- Fresh basil is key to this dish. Add it at the end to preserve its flavor.
